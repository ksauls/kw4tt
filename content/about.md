# About Me! #

Hi!  I'm Kevin Sauls, KW4TT.

I have been an amateur radio operator since late 2018 and achieved my Extra ticket in mid-2026. I've been an computer geek for far longer: I started with a Commodore 128 way back in 1986.  I've been through a LOT of computers since then.

This site is all about my exploration of the *many* aspects of amateur radio.
